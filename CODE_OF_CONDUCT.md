# ZLife Community Code of Conduct

ZLife should be a professional, useful, and safe place to build.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, product decisions, and reviews.
- Critique work, not people.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels.
- Keep technical disagreements evidence-based.
- Protect user privacy, customer data, credentials, and production systems.
- Report security issues privately instead of exploiting or publicly disclosing them.
- Give accurate attribution and do not claim work, titles, results, customers, revenue, or status that have not been verified.
- Avoid spam, harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, or deliberate disruption.

## Contribution integrity

Do not inflate contribution metrics through meaningless commits, artificial issues, duplicate PRs, automated noise, or coordinated scoring manipulation. Contributor status and any future reward weight must be based on verified useful work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, close, hide, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations, and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors, users, or the project.

Serious security, privacy, fraud, abuse, or legal concerns should be escalated to Ziepher Tech maintainers through the private security-reporting process described in `SECURITY.md`.
